PADI Speciality Diver

Each PADI speciality certification gives you an introduction to a specific area of diving.

It can be a good way both learn skills needed for specific dives & also re-ignite your love for diving after a period of time.

Speciality certifications also contribute towards the Master Scuba Diver rating.
