Continue Diving

Congratulations on your existing certification!

What’s next? It’s important to keep your skills fresh & expand your knowledge to really enjoy diving.

There are many possible options within the PADI system, the 2 main tracks covering core diving skills & speciality diving skills.

Core track

There are 3 key levels within the core certification track;

  1. Open water diver - maximum depth of 18meters
  2. Advanced open water diver - maximum depth of 30meters
  3. Rescue diver - learn how to recognise, prevent & manage problems

After rescue diver you can either continue on the recreational track, up to master scuba diver, or enter the professional track, starting with Divemaster.

Specialities

Speciality diver courses give you the opportunity to try new types of diving & learn the specific skills required in those environments.

It can be a good way both learn skills needed for specific dives & also re-ignite your love for diving after a period of time.

During the advanced open water course you will already experience an introduction into 5 speciality areas, however there are a lot more possibilities.

TecRec

For experienced divers, there are a number of TecRec course options, which will expand your knowledge & skillset beyond that attainable by recreational divers.

Why Dive with Damian?

DwD is focused on providing high quality training, with staff certified in many areas including TecRec.

We will focus on your goals, ensuring you get the most relevant training & are comfortable diving in each area.
