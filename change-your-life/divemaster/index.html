PADI Divemaster

During the Divemaster course you will work closely with a PADI instructor, expanding your dive knowledge and refining your skills up to a professional level.

As the first PADI professional level, your leadership skills & professionalism will be key to success.

The theory will cover multiple aspects including:

  • Roles, characteristics & responsibilities of a Divemaster
  • Supervising certified divers
  • Assisting with student divers
  • Dive theory

In-water training will include:

  • Development of water skills; swimming, snorkeling etc
  • Dive skill refinement & demonstration
  • Dive site mapping
  • Multiple dive scenarios including deep diving
Course Content
  • 5 knowledge development segments
  • 5 water skill exercises
  • Diver rescue evaluation
  • 24 dive skill evaluations
  • 5 practical skills
  • 5 workshops
  • 4 practical assessments
  • Professionalism
Course Prerequisites
  • At least 18 years old
  • At least 40 Logged dives
  • EFR Primary & Secondary Care
  • Medical clearance for diving
