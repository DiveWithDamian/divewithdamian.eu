EFR Secondary Care (First Aid)

EFR Secondary Care (First Aid) covers how to provide care for someone in need.

The theory will cover multiple aspects including:

  • Contact Statements
  • Secondary Assessments & Care
  • Injuries vs Illnesses
  • Signs vs Symptoms

After completing the theory, you will practice a number of skills including:

  • Injury Assessment
  • Illness Assessment
  • Bandaging
  • Splinting

Completing the course with the final exam & a number of realistic scenarios.

  • 4 skill development segments
