PADI Tec Deep

During the Tec Deep package you will complete the Tec 40, Tec 45 & Tec 50 sub courses.

This course will transform you from an experienced recreational diver into a safe technical diver, capable of making accelerated decompression dives up to 50 meters.

Course Content
  • 9 knowledge development segments
  • 9 practical applications
  • 12 training dives
  • 3 exams
Course Prerequisites
  • At least 18 years old
  • At least 100 dives, with 20 using EANx below 18 meters & 15 below 30 meters
Course Price

From € 1299

Includes
  • Course manual
  • Gases per course outline
  • Renting of double set & stages per course outline
Excludes
  • Log book
  • Personal Tec equipment (wing, harness, regulators, torch etc)
