During the Tec Deep courses (Tec 40, Tec 45, Tec 50), or Discover Tec experience, there are strict equipment requirements.

Discover Tec

  • Primary and secondary regulators for back mounted double manifolds, one with two metre hose for air sharing and one with SPG.
  • Tec BCD (wing) and harness
  • Dive computer and/or depth gauge and timing device with tables.
  • Exposure suit appropriate for environment and dive duration.
  • Inflatable signal tube, whistle and/or other visual and audible surface signaling devices.
  • Reel and DSMB - A suitable DSMB has sufficient buoyancy to help steady a diver during a drifting decompression, and is unlikely to spill when deployed from the underwater.
  • Knife/cutting device and back up
  • Slate
  • Backup mask
  • Compass
  • Lights as required by the dive environment

Tec 40

  • Two complete regulators, one with a two-metre hose for air sharing and one with SPG.
  • One stage regulator
  • BCD with D-rings or other attachment points for a stage/deco cylinder.
  • Two dive computers, or one computer with a backup timer and depth gauge with dive tables.
  • Exposure suit appropriate for environment and dive duration.
  • Inflatable signal tube, whistle and/or other visual and audible surface signaling devices.
  • Reel and DSMB - A suitable DSMB has sufficient buoyancy to help steady a diver during a drifting decompression, and is unlikely to spill when deployed from the underwater.
  • Knife/cutting device and back up
  • Slate
  • Backup mask
  • Compass
  • Lights as required by the dive environment
  • Backup buoyancy control (double wing or dry suit)

Tec 45

  • Primary and secondary regulators for back mounted double manifolds, one with two metre hose for air sharing and one with SPG.
  • One stage regulator
  • Tec BCD (wing) and harness
  • Two multi-gas enriched air capable dive computers, or one multi-gas enriched air computer and a back up single gas computer with dive tables, or one single gas enriched air computer and a backup timer and depth gauge with dive tables.
  • Exposure suit appropriate for environment and dive duration.
  • Inflatable signal tube, whistle and/or other visual and audible surface signaling devices.
  • Reel and DSMB - A suitable DSMB has sufficient buoyancy to help steady a diver during a drifting decompression, and is unlikely to spill when deployed from the underwater.
  • Knife/cutting device and back up
  • Slate
  • Backup mask
  • Compass
  • Lights as required by the dive environment
  • Backup buoyancy control (double wing or dry suit)

Tec 50

  • All requirements from Tec 45
  • Two stage regulators
